# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Three audiences, all confirmed as primary:

- **General public arriving from a shared link.** Usually a single visit, prompted by a link preview, curious about their own number.
- **Richard Crane's audience.** Listeners of the Inventing Fire with AI podcast, MILL5 contacts, and LinkedIn followers, meeting the tool as part of Rich's work.
- **People interested in living on purpose** who may also follow the Days on Purpose app suite at daysonpurpose.com.

The job is the same for all three: enter three facts, see an honest estimate of the days ahead, and leave with a reason to use today well.

## Product Purpose

Days on Purpose estimates a visitor's remaining "purpose days" from their birth date, country, and gender, using published life expectancy at birth. It reframes that remaining time as a resource to invest, not as a mortality countdown.

Success after a result:

1. The visitor reflects and picks one purposeful action for today.
2. The visitor shares the page.

## Positioning

A free, no-account, browser-only calculator that turns a statistical average into a prompt for action. It does not collect data, predict an individual lifespan, or use fear.

## Operating Context

- Static page served by GitHub Pages at `https://chiefinnovator.github.io/daysonpurpose/` from the root of `main`.
- Visitors arrive on phones and desktops, often from a social link preview (`og-image.png`).
- Inputs persist in the browser's Local Storage so a return visit restores them.
- Work flows `feature/*` → `develop` → `main`. Merging to `main` publishes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Inputs: birth date, country (dataset list), gender (`male` or `female`).
- Outputs: days, weeks, months, years, 40-day blocks, 100-day blocks, a live ticker (years through seconds) and a total-seconds figure, a purpose message, and a source footnote.
- Each unit is computed from remaining years using 365.2425 days per year.
- No backend, no account, no analytics. All computation runs in the browser.
- Built per `docs/designs/days-on-purpose-v2.md`: ES modules with no build step, UN World Population Prospects 2024 data (2023 estimates), no third-party network requests, country suggested from browser language.
- Terminology: "purpose days", "days for your journey", "invest today". The visible and ARIA text avoids "countdown", "remaining", and "left".
- License: PolyForm Strict 1.0.0 (`LICENSE`).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Days on Purpose**. Tagline: "Turn time into meaningful action."
- **Standalone identity.** The calculator does not visually match or link to the daysonpurpose.com app suite.
- Voice: neutral, supportive, purpose-first. No fear-based copy. Celebrate visitors past the average: "You've surpassed the average—keep investing in what matters."
- Disclaimer must stay visible: "Statistical estimate, not an individual prediction."
- Attribution: author Richard Crane. Project footers credit MILL5 and mention VS Code, GitHub, and Microsoft MVP. Never use self-referential "Powered by Days on Purpose".

## Product Principles

1. **Purpose over mortality.** Every number points to action today, never toward dread.
2. **Honest about the estimate.** Name the data source and year, and say plainly that it is a statistical average.
3. **Nothing leaves the browser.** No accounts, tracking, or third-party requests.
4. **Three fields, one answer.** The visitor reaches a result with no friction and no sign-up.
5. **Worth sharing.** The result and the link preview should make someone want to pass it on.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in both light and dark color schemes.
- Full keyboard use, labelled fields, an ARIA live region for results, and focus moved to the result on calculate.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
